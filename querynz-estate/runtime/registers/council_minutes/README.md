# registers.council_minutes

Step 6.3: council minutes from the councils on one meeting platform (InfoCouncil), captured and held with no read of
attendance or resolutions. Filled by councils-1. The table is the meeting list from each council's own listing; the
minutes PDFs are landed by hash and never read. `spec.md` says what it owns, reads and publishes; `position/` holds the
draft legal position the integrator moves to `docs/legal/positions/`.
