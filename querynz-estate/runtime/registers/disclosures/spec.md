Two dates, both from the Commission's table: `received_on`, the day the Commission received the declaration (the table leads with it; kept from the original through an amendment, and in the key), and `given_on`, the day the party received the gift (printed after the amount; NULL where a 2008–2022 declaration itemises several gifts, whose own dates are in `pub.disclosure_items`). A declaration belongs to the year of `given_on`, never of `received_on`: measured on all 520 held, 8 gifts were declared to the Commission in a later year (5 of 276 from 2023 on), and bucketing by the gift equals the frozen site on 15 of 16 party-years. A linked scan with no total read is a catch-log line (`set_aside`, stage `form`, its hash and pages: needs a reading, ruling 62), counted apart from the listing's reconciliation because its row stands. Ruling 66: a declaration's itemised gifts against its printed amount reconcile to the cent (`equal`), or below $100 (`reconciled`, the row's `issue` naming the signed difference and the parts, counted by `check.py`), and at $100 or more do not (`differs`, shown as now); the printed amount is the one published. The form's total against the table is one figure printed twice, not parts, and stays to the cent
