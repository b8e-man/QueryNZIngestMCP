# Council procurement off GETS, on the board

Not held. Councils' own lists of tenders and awarded contracts are kept out by their terms (Clutha: no
commercial copying; Tauranga: non-commercial use only; Dunedin: use on request), and the platforms councils use
ask for a login (VendorPanel) or answered with a challenge (TenderLink, Queenstown Lakes). The pane says "not held:
councils' own procurement lists are barred by their terms or behind a login" and shows the watch's last answer
per page and its date. Councils' awards posted on GETS are on the award notices pane (`pub.award_notices`).
