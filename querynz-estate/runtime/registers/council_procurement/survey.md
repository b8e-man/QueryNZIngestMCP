# Where councils publish tenders and awards off GETS: the survey of 22 October 2026 (procurement-1)

Through the fetch gate, 20:00 to 20:20 NZDT, serial, 5 s apart (Clutha's 7 s crawl delay honoured): each council's
home page, then the first of its own links whose words say tender, procurement or contract, and the platforms that
page names. 45 councils asked; two more (Thames-Coromandel, Western Bay of Plenty) were refused by the gate before
the position named their hosts and were not asked. The gate's ledger holds every request.

## The collected file first

Councils are eligible agencies on GETS. The ministry's award notices (lane `tenders`) hold 6,545 current notices
from 76 councils. Of the councils below that list awards elsewhere, Dunedin (429), Queenstown Lakes (331),
Waimakariri (174) and Clutha (61) are in it; Tauranga is not.

## What would add rows

Dunedin's quarterly PDFs are the largest own-site list, and its words invite a request; Clutha's 17 and Tauranga's
current tenders are small. Each would be a letter; ruling 136 (3 November 2026): none is sent, the lists are stored and off every schedule.
