# registers.council_procurement

Step 6.3: council procurement on platforms other than GETS (use cases 9 and 10). Surveyed 22 October 2026 by
procurement-1 (`survey.md`): most councils post on GETS, whose award notices the tenders lane already holds (76
councils); the rest use TenderLink (a login or a challenge), VendorPanel (registered suppliers only) or their own
sites, and every own-site list found is barred for commercial use or offered on request. So the lane is a monthly
watch of the four pages whose words decide it (`capture.py`, `read.py`, `check.py`), its position page, and the
staging contract kept with nothing to load. The ways in are letters, which are Arthur's (queue
`whether-to-ask-councils-for-their-award-lists`).
